<section class="home-intro-card" markdown="1">
I am currently pursuing a [Master of Behavioral and Decision Sciences](https://www.lps.upenn.edu/degree-programs/mbds) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/), where my research is supervised by [Prof. Lorin Hitt](https://oid.wharton.upenn.edu/profile/lhitt/) of the [Wharton School](https://www.wharton.upenn.edu/). In 2025, I completed a [dual-degree program in Information Management and Information Systems](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/study/undergraduate/information-management-and-information-systems), earning a BSc (Hons) from the [University of Liverpool](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/) and a BMan from [Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU)](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en). My research has been presented at major conferences, including PACIS, AMCIS, the INFORMS Annual Meeting, and the AOM Annual Meeting.

My research lies in the economics of information systems. I study digital platforms and emerging information technologies, especially artificial intelligence, and the industries and markets in which they are developed and used. My current work examines how information design, incentives, and algorithmic allocation shape the decisions of users, firms, and platforms. More broadly, I am interested in how changes in technology and information reshape economic behavior, competitive strategy, and market structure. Empirically, I combine econometric analysis with laboratory and field experiments, drawing on text analysis, machine learning, and LLM-based annotation.
</section>
