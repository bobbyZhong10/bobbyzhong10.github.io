<section class="home-intro-card" markdown="1">
I am a master’s student in [Behavioral and Decision Sciences](https://www.lps.upenn.edu/degree-programs/mbds) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/). My research is supervised by [Prof. Lorin Hitt](https://oid.wharton.upenn.edu/profile/lhitt/) at the [Wharton School](https://www.wharton.upenn.edu/). I hold a Bachelor of Science with Honours from the [University of Liverpool](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/) and a Bachelor of Management from [Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU)](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en), both earned through a [dual-degree program in Information Management and Information Systems](https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/study/undergraduate/information-management-and-information-systems).

My research is in the economics of information systems, with a focus on digital platforms and emerging technologies, especially artificial intelligence. My work spans two streams: how social media, online word of mouth, and AI-generated content shape user behavior and economic outcomes, and how algorithmic pricing and allocation shape firm strategy, competition, and labor markets. Empirically, I combine econometric analysis with natural, field, and laboratory experiments, drawing on text analysis and machine learning. I have presented my research at premier conferences and workshops, including PACIS, AMCIS, the INFORMS Annual Meeting, and the AOM Annual Meeting.
